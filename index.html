📖 Pour-dle – Game Guide

Pour-dle is a bar-themed word guessing game. Each day a new secret 5-letter drinking word is selected — your mission is to find it in 6 rounds or fewer using colour-coded clues.

How to Play

  1. Type any 5-letter word using the on-screen taps or your keyboard.
  2. Press Enter / Pour It to submit your guess.
  3. 🟩 Green = correct letter, correct position.
  4. 🟨 Yellow = correct letter, wrong position.
  5. ⬛ Grey = letter not in the word.
  6. Solve the word in 6 tries to earn XP and level up!

Modes

  • 📅 Daily — One shared word per day. Compare with friends!
  • 🔁 Continuous — Unlimited rounds, earn XP with every win.
